DATAEKO × meshIQ Partner Dashboard Handoff

Enterprise assessment platform designed to discover, quantify, and report the economic modernization opportunity for enterprise IBM MQ middleware environments using meshIQ observability and automation solutions.

Branch: meshiq-handoff Code Baseline: dev (e2ee663) ✓ Golden Master: 10/10 PASS ✓ RBAC / Isolation: PASS PDF Engine: Chromium Headless

1. Project Overview

The DATAEKO × meshIQ Partner Dashboard is an enterprise software solution built jointly by DATAEKO.AI and meshIQ. The application enables enterprise customers and consultants to evaluate complex IBM MQ middleware estates, analyze operational friction, and generate defensible economic valuations for modernization.

The platform combines a multi-step interactive intake wizard (Sections A–G), a deterministic Decimal-precision Python calculation engine, real-time executive dashboard visualizations, publication-grade 3-page executive PDF deliverables, and an asynchronous dual-branded email distribution pipeline.

2. Purpose of the DATAEKO × meshIQ Assessment Platform

Enterprises operating legacy IBM MQ environments face three primary compounding costs:

  • High Routine Administrative Overhead: Manual queue provisioning, channel cycling, credential rotation, and certificate audits consume thousands of specialist engineering hours annually.
  • Prolonged Mean-Time-To-Resolution (MTTR): Lack of granular cross-queue visibility results in blind spots during incident triage, causing costly Sev-1/Sev-2 downtime.
  • Compliance & Operational Risk: Missing message tracking and manual log gathering create significant regulatory exposure in banking, healthcare, and critical logistics.

The dashboard transforms discovery inputs into executive-ready metrics (3-Year Total Savings, 5-Year Cumulative ROI, Payback Months, and Hours Reclaimed) grounded in authoritative industry economic benchmarks.

3. Customer Workflow (Client Experience)

Customers access the platform via client accounts (role CUSTOMER_USER). Upon authenticated login, the customer workflow proceeds as follows:

1. Client Logs In (http://localhost:3000/login)
↓
2. Lands Directly in Assessment Wizard (Section A)
↓
3. Completes Sections A through G (Q01–Q22 Manually)
↓
4. Real-time Auto-Save & Resume Validation Across Sessions
↓
5. Review Summary Step (Visual Integrity Validation)
↓
6. Final Assessment Submission (Immutable Lock)
↓
7. Read-Only Summary & Instant Confirmation Email Receipt

4. Consultant Workflow

Users authenticated with the CONSULTANT role land on the Consultant Portfolio Workspace. Consultants have elevated read and calculation access across all customer organizations within the tenant:

  • Portfolio Overview: View submitted, calculated, and draft assessments across all client accounts.
  • Economic Calculation Runs: Trigger the calculation engine on submitted customer assessments to generate immutable CalculationSnapshot records.
  • Deliverable Distribution: Download executive PDF deliverables, review calculation audit logs, and trigger executive presentation packs.

5. Admin Workflow (Platform & Partner Admins)

Users authenticated with PLATFORM_ADMIN or PARTNER_ADMIN roles have access to the Admin Governance Workspace:

  • Customer Registry: Provision new customer organizations, update organizational metadata, and inspect client activity.
  • User Lifecycle Governance: Provision new users, issue secure invitation tokens, reset credentials, and activate/deactivate accounts.
  • Audit Trail Inspection: Real-time searchable log of all authentication events, assessment creations, response updates, and calculation executions.

7. Calculation Engine Architecture

Located in backend/app/calculation_engine/, the engine is implemented in pure, decoupled Python using decimal.Decimal to eliminate floating-point drift.

The engine takes normalized Q01–Q22 inputs and executes sequentially through isolated calculation streams: 

Intake Inputs → Engine Orchestrator → Stream A → Stream B → Stream C → Stream D → Aggregate Valuation → CalculationSnapshot

8. Economic Metrics & Value Streams

The valuation model decomposes economic impact into 4 distinct streams:

  • Stream A — Administrative Efficiency: Labor hours reclaimed through automated provisioning and configuration validation. Formulated from Q04 (exact quarterly hours × 4) and Q06 (loaded hourly rate).
  • Stream B — Incident MTTR & Downtime Reduction: Sev-1/Sev-2 operational savings derived from incident frequency (Q08), MTTR reduction factor (Q09), and cost of downtime (Q10).
  • Stream C — Testing & Release Velocity: Engineering labor reclaimed by accelerating middleware integration testing cycles (Q15, Q17).
  • Stream D — Compliance & Audit Cost Avoidance: Reduction in audit preparation labor and regulatory penalty risk (Q21, Q22).

9. CalculationSnapshot Immutability

Calculations are never recalculated dynamically on report load. Instead, the backend persists an immutable CalculationSnapshot record upon calculation execution:

  • Cryptographic Integrity: Includes input hash, model version tag (1.0.0), and complete serialized JSON payload.
  • Auditable Provenance: Executive dashboards, CSV deliverables, and PDF reports read strictly from the snapshot. Historical records never shift when underlying constants or algorithms evolve in future releases.

11. Scenario Functionality

The engine models three standardized modernization trajectories:

  • Baseline (Authoritative): Default benchmark efficiency capture aligned with Golden Master tests.
  • Conservative: Adjusted lower capture multipliers for phased migrations or highly risk-averse environments.
  • Accelerated: Full-scale automation capture reflecting rapid enterprise-wide meshIQ adoption.

13. Email Workflow & Notification Pipeline

Managed by backend/app/services/email_service.py:

  • Client Confirmation: Automatically sent upon assessment submission with subject: DATAEKO × meshIQ Assessment Submission Confirmation: {assessment_title}.
  • Dual-Branded HTML: Responsive, high-contrast, inline-styled transactional email template.
  • Safety Invariant: In development, email delivery operates in test-mode / headless state (EMAIL_ENABLED=False) preventing accidental outbound email transmission.

14. Authentication Architecture

Implemented in backend/app/core/security.py and backend/app/api/v1/auth.py:

  • HTTP-Only Cookies: Session tokens are issued in secure, HTTP-only, SameSite=Lax cookies preventing XSS token exfiltration.
  • HMAC-SHA256 JWTs: Signed tokens contain subject, tenant ID, customer ID, role, and auth_version.
  • Instant Invalidation: Password resets and admin deactivations increment User.auth_version, instantly invalidating active sessions across all devices.

15. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

The platform enforces 5 discrete roles:

Role Landing Experience Permissions
PLATFORM_ADMIN Admin Governance Full cross-tenant system administration, user management, audit logs.
PARTNER_ADMIN Admin Governance Tenant-level administration, customer onboarding, user management.
CONSULTANT Consultant Portfolio Cross-customer assessment read, calculate, and deliverable download.
CUSTOMER_ADMIN Customer Admin Manage client users within own customer organization; assessment oversight.
CUSTOMER_USER Assessment Wizard Fill out Q01–Q22 questionnaire, save draft, review, and submit own assessment.

16. Multi-Tenant & Customer Isolation

All database tables enforce foreign key bindings to tenants.id. Customer-level users (CUSTOMER_ADMIN and CUSTOMER_USER) are additionally bound to customers.id:

  • IDOR Defense: API endpoints verify caller customer binding; cross-organization requests return 404 Not Found.
  • User Directory Scoping: GET /api/v1/users strictly filters users to User.customer_id == current_user.customer_id.

17. Frontend Architecture

  • Framework: Next.js 14+ (App Router) with TypeScript.
  • State Management: React hooks with modular Context (AuthContext).
  • Styling: Tailwind CSS utility tokens combined with custom meshIQ enterprise visual identity.
  • Routing: Dynamic client-side role dispatching in frontend/src/app/page.tsx.

18. Backend Architecture

  • Framework: FastAPI (Python 3.11+, tested on 3.14).
  • Async Database ORM: SQLAlchemy 2.0 with aiosqlite (local) and asyncpg (PostgreSQL).
  • Validation: Pydantic v2 schemas for all request/response models.
  • Observability: Structured JSON logging with request correlation IDs (X-Request-ID).

19. Database Architecture & Schema

Managed via asynchronous Alembic migrations:

  • tenants: Multi-tenant partitioning roots.
  • customers: Enterprise client organizations.
  • users: Authenticated user accounts with role and customer bindings.
  • assessments: Assessment instances with lifecycle statuses (DRAFT, SUBMITTED, CALCULATED).
  • assessment_responses: Serialized Q01–Q22 discovery answers.
  • calculation_snapshots: Immutable point-in-time calculation outputs.
  • audit_events: Immutable audit log for all system and security events.
  • user_credential_tokens: SHA-256 hashed invitation and password reset tokens.

⚠️ 25. Protected Business Logic (Mandatory Safeguards)

CRITICAL ARCHITECTURAL DIRECTIVE FOR FUTURE DEVELOPERS The economic calculation engine, question catalog mappings, and valuation methodologies represent approved commercial intellectual property. Future engineering tasks must NOT alter the protected logic listed below without explicit, written stakeholder approval.

Strictly Protected Entities & Invariants:

  • Q01–Q22 Semantics & Catalog Mappings: Question codes, unit dimensions, scoring scales, and default overrides (such as Q20) must not be renamed, reordered, or removed.
  • Q04 Exact Numeric Intake: Q04 captures exact quarterly administration hours. It must never be replaced with synthetic ranges or approximations.
  • Calculation Formulas & Constants: Formulas for Streams A, B, C, and D, loaded labor multipliers, incident risk factors, and payback math are mathematically binding.
  • Decimal Arithmetic & Rounding: All calculations must use Python's decimal.Decimal to prevent binary floating-point drift.
  • CalculationSnapshot Immutability: Historical snapshots must never be overwritten, recomputed, or modified after creation.
  • Golden Master Test Invariant: All 10 Golden Master tests in test_golden_masters.py must pass 10/10.
  • Tenant & Customer Isolation: Foreign key scoping (tenant_id, customer_id) and IDOR defenses must remain enforced on all API routes.

If any future business requirement appears to conflict with these invariants, the developer must STOP and obtain formal stakeholder approval before proceeding.
